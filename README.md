# Salman Khan

**Software Engineer | Founder & CEO, QuivixDigital | Researcher**

I am a software engineer, researcher, and technology entrepreneur based in Konya, Türkiye, currently pursuing a B.Sc. in Software Engineering at Konya Technical University.

I specialize in designing, developing, and deploying software systems across backend engineering, mobile development, distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, and artificial intelligence.

As the founder of **QuivixDigital**, I oversee the complete engineering lifecycle of software products, from architecture and implementation to deployment, infrastructure management, and production operations.

My work combines practical software engineering with independent research in artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and intelligent systems.
